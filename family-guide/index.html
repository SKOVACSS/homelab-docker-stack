A few things worth knowing

  • Bookmark your favorites, or add them to your phone's home screen (in Safari or Chrome, look for "Add to Home Screen") so they open like a normal app with one tap.
  • Keep your passwords in the Passwords app itself once you're set up — that way you only ever have to remember one, and it can fill the rest in for you.
  • If something won't load, it may just be restarting — wait a minute and try again before worrying.
  • Never share your login for any of these with anyone outside the family, the same as you wouldn't share a bank password.
  • Questions are always welcome — ask whoever set this up. There's no such thing as a silly question here.
