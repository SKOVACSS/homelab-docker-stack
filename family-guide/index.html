Good to know

One family login for (almost) everything

Your family account (Authentik) opens most apps. Whenever you see “Sign in with Authentik”, use it. A few apps have their own username and password instead: Photos, Movies & Shows, Music, Books, Passwords and Read Later. The setup steps for each app say which login to use.

An app says it's “starting” or won't load

Some apps (AI Assistant, PDF Tools, Jellyfin) go to sleep when nobody is using them and wake up on the first visit, which takes about a minute. The Home Page shows a dot on every app: green means ready. If an app stays grey or red for more than a few minutes, let whoever runs the server know.

Does it work away from home?

Yes. Every app works anywhere you have internet, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. The only exception is the photo slideshow for the TV, which works at home only.

Turn on 2-step login

It stops anyone who guesses your password. Open Your Family Account (below) → Settings → MFA Devices → Enroll → TOTP, and scan the code with an authenticator app (2FAS, or the one built into Bitwarden).

“Add to Home Screen” (for apps with no store app)
  • iPhone / iPad: open the app in Safari → tap the Share button (square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome → tap ⋮ (top right) → Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or just bookmark it.

It then opens full-screen from its own icon, like a normal app.

Staying safe
  • Never share your login with anyone outside the family, same as a bank password.
  • Keep your passwords in the Passwords app so you only need to remember one.
  • Questions are always welcome. There's no such thing as a silly question here.
